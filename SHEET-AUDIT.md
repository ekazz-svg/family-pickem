# Sheet audit: family sheet vs. official final scores

Every pick in the weekly screenshots was checked against ESPN's official final score for that game. The color of every cell (green = sheet gave credit) was measured from the screenshot pixels, not eyeballed. Where a sheet has a totals row, it equals the number of green cells in every case, so the errors all come from cell colors.

**Official results are what the dashboard uses.** Sheet colors and totals are only compared here.
